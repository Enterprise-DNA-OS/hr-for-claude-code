# Research behind this build

- Employment Hero pricing, AU and NZ pages, checked 10 October 2026: https://employmenthero.com/au/pricing/ and https://employmenthero.com/nz/pricing/. HR Essentials and HR Engage list a per-user price with a minimum of 10 users; HR Elite and Employment Unlimited are custom quotes; add-ons such as HR Advisory and LMS Plus are priced per employee a month. Payroll is in the Employment Unlimited tier or the Managed Payroll add-on.
- Employment Hero export, checked 10 October 2026: https://help.employmenthero.com/hc/en-au/articles/17427183987983 (People > Import Employees > Advanced Import Employees, with existing and terminated users ticked, downloads a template filled with every employee's details).
- The rules behind each record check, with sources: docs/compliance.md.
