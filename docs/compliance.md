# Record checks and their sources

`/compliance` (`npm run hr -- compliance`) checks the employment records against the rules below and lists every record that fails one. It finds records that are missing, late or inconsistent. It does not decide whether a trial period will hold, whether a dismissal is justified or whether an exception applies to a contract. Those calls belong to the business and its employment lawyer. Sources checked 10 October 2026.

| Rule | What the check finds | Source and limit |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-WRITTEN-AGREEMENT | A current NZ employee with no signed employment agreement on file | [Employment Relations Act 2000](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2000/0024/latest/DLM58317.html) ss63A, 64 and 65: every employee has a written agreement, and the employer keeps a signed copy (or the copy it offered). Employment New Zealand, [employment agreements](https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/employment-agreements). The check looks for a signed date, not at what the agreement says. |
| NZ-TRIAL-PERIOD | An NZ trial period longer than 90 days, with no signed agreement, or signed on or after the start date | Employment Relations Act 2000 s67A: a trial period of up to 90 days, agreed in writing in the employment agreement before the employee starts work. Since 23 December 2023 any employer can use one ([MBIE](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/about/news/extending-the-availability-of-90-day-trial-periods-to-all-employers)). The rule also excludes anyone previously employed by the same employer; record that by clearing the trial. |
| NZ-AEWV-TRIAL | A trial period in the agreement of an AEWV holder at an accredited employer | Immigration New Zealand, [AEWV and 90-day trial periods](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/accredited-employer-work-visa-aewv-and-90-day-trial-periods/): from 29 October 2023 accredited employers must not use trial periods in AEWV employment agreements, and risk accreditation by doing so. Set `accredited_employer` on the employer and the visa type `AEWV`. |
| AU-FWIS | A current Australian employee with no Fair Work Information Statement recorded, a week after starting | [Fair Work Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/latest/text) s125: given before, or as soon as possible after, a new employee starts. Fair Work Ombudsman, [FWIS](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/national-employment-standards/fair-work-information-statement). |
| AU-CEIS | An Australian casual not given the Casual Employment Information Statement when it last fell due | Fair Work Act s125A and the Fair Work Ombudsman's [CEIS page](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/ceis): at the start for every casual; then, from 26 August 2024, again at 12 months for a small business employer, and at 6 months, 12 months and every 12 months after for any other employer. Small business means fewer than 15 employees (s23); this check counts everyone except casuals, because casuals count only when regular and systematic. Change `v_employer_size` with `/customise` if yours are. A statement given up to 30 days before it is due counts. |
| AU-FTCIS | An Australian fixed-term employee with no Fixed Term Contract Information Statement recorded | Fair Work Act s125B and the Fair Work Ombudsman's [FTCIS page](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/ftcis): given to every employee entering a fixed-term contract, from 6 December 2023. |
| AU-FIXED-TERM-LIMIT | An Australian fixed-term contract that started on or after 6 December 2023 and runs past two years, or has been renewed more than once, with no exception recorded | Fair Work Act s333E and the Fair Work Ombudsman's [fixed-term contract employees page](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/starting-employment/types-of-employees/fixed-term-contract-employees). Exceptions exist (specialised skills for a distinct task, training arrangements, peak demand, high income earners, some government funding, and others). Record the one relied on with `update --fixed-term-exception="..."` after advice; the check then skips the contract. It does not test the consecutive-contracts rule. |
| RIGHT-TO-WORK | A current employee working on a visa with no expiry date recorded, or one that has expired | NZ [Immigration Act 2009](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2009/0051/latest/DLM1440303.html) s350: an employer must not employ a person who is not entitled to work. AU Migration Act 1958 s245AB: allowing an unlawful non-citizen to work, or a visa holder to work in breach of a condition. Check status in VisaView (NZ) or [VEVO](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/check-conditions-online) (AU); this check reads the expiry you recorded, not the government system. `/attention` warns 60 days before expiry. |
| REQUIRED-CERTIFICATION | A licence, ticket or certificate marked required that has expired | NZ [Health and Safety at Work Act 2015](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2015/0070/latest/DLM5976660.html) s36 and the model [WHS Act](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/model-whs-laws) s19: the business must ensure, so far as reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers, including that people operating plant are competent. Which tickets a role needs is the business's call; mark them required. |

## What is not checked here

- **Pay, leave accrual, KiwiSaver, super, Payday Filing and STP.** Those stay in payroll. Balances come across as a snapshot so the leave liability can be read, nothing more.
- **Record retention.** NZ wage, time and holiday records are kept for six years (Employment Relations Act s130, Holidays Act 2003 s81); Australian time and wages records for seven ([Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/paying-wages/record-keeping)). This system has no delete command, so records of people who have left stay put.
- **Modern awards and collective agreements.** Which award applies, and its rates and rosters, belong in payroll and in the agreement.

The attention list (`/attention`) adds operating checks with no legal source: leave requests waiting, reviews overdue or never booked, trials and probation ending without a review, tickets and visas expiring, policies not acknowledged, case steps overdue, onboarding and offboarding tasks late, large annual leave balances and people leaving soon. The thresholds live in the `settings` table.
